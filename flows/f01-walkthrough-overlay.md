## Essence
A person walks and shows an object. On top of and between the shots: text, callouts, images,
transitions, "how it could be" inserts. The speaker's voice runs continuously.

## Steps
| # | Step | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | skeleton | Meaning blocks of the speech ("living room", "mounting joint", "price"), strong shots |
| 2 | beats | Hook (the best view or phrase goes first), 3–5 blocks, CTA. Target length per platform |
| 3 | select + cut | Fragments per block; pauses and "um"s are cut on word boundaries |
| 4 | overlay | On trigger words: a callout with an arrow to a detail, a specification badge (a fact from product-base!), before/after (wipe), PiP, a full-screen insert of 1.5–3 s under the voice (J/L-cut) |
| 5 | image_gen *(opt.)* | If a "how it could be" insert is needed and there is no image → f04 as a sub-route |
| 6 | subtitles | Word by word, key words highlighted |
| 7 | grade + audio_mix | Uniform color, −14 LUFS, light music under the voice |
| 8 | render → review_video | |

## Techniques
- Pattern interrupt every 2–4 s: change of scale (crop 1.2–1.5×), a callout, an insert.
- Do not cover the face at the moment of the key phrase.
- 9:16 safe zones.
- Callouts on shaky footage: stabilize first (`vidstab`), then track with keyframes.
