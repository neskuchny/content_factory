## Precondition
Written consent for the face and voice in `brand/consents.md`. Without it the flow does not start.

## Preparation (once)
- Reference video: 2–5 minutes, even diffused light, a quiet room, eyes to the camera, the face
  fully visible, a static background. Store in `brand/people/<name>/`.
- Voice: an instant clone needs 1–2 minutes of clean speech; a professional one needs 30+ minutes.
  Check pronunciation and stress on a test text in the client's language
  (for `ru` clients: check word stress on a Russian test text).

## Steps
| # | Step | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | beats + text | Short phrases, natural speech, no bureaucratese |
| 2 | voice | Voice clone → track |
| 3 | Visual | **Option A (more realistic):** real footage of the person + Sync Lipsync 3 to the new track. **Option B:** HeyGen Avatar V / photo + audio |
| 4 | Hide the stillness | The avatar stays in frame no longer than 3–5 s at a time → B-roll (object, product, process), change of scale, subtitles, badges |
| 5 | label_ai | "Made with AI" in the frame and the caption |
| 6 | render → review_video | Check lips, stress, naturalness |
