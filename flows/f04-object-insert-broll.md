## Essence
A person shows a site → insert: this same site, and the structure appears and "grows" on it
(motion) → then a regular video. The insert is a separate piece, not in the shot with the person
(for "in the shot" see f05).

## Steps
| # | Step | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | skeleton | The best wide shot of the site: static camera, good light, the building spot visible |
| 2 | extract_frame | Frame A — the empty site (without a person, if possible) |
| 3 | image_edit | Frame B = A + the structure from product references. "Match perspective, sun direction and shadows; keep everything else identical; preserve product geometry and profiles as in reference; no text" |
| 4 | review_still | Product geometry as in product-base; scale relative to the site; shadows in the same direction; **outside the object A and B match** (otherwise edit again) |
| 5 | flf_video | Kling 3.0 Omni (Frames): start = A, end = B. "Static locked-off camera, the structure frame assembles piece by piece, panels slide in, timelapse feel". 5–8 s, sound off. 3–4 attempts |
| 6 | overlay *(opt.)* | A motion layer in code: dimension lines, material callouts (facts from product-base) |
| 7 | label_ai | Mandatory |
| 8 | seam | Insert into the video: a cut on a word ("right here there will be…") or a whip pan + whoosh. The speaker's voice can carry on over it (J-cut) |
| 9 | grade (match the AI piece to the source color, shared grain) → audio_mix → render → review_video | |

## Variants
- "Life" after the appearance: B → a short i2v shot "evening, light inside, people at a table" (3–5 s).
- No video source: A = a photo of the site from the client.
