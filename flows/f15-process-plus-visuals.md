## Essence
We show the real process and dilute it with created pieces: AI visualizations,
3D animation, diagrams, stock.

## Steps
| # | Step | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | skeleton | Process stages, close-ups of details |
| 2 | beats | "Why this matters to the client" at each stage (benefit, not process for its own sake) |
| 3 | select + cut | Rhythm of 1.5–3 s per shot, speed up long operations (timelapse `setpts`) |
| 4 | Additions | A diagram or a breakdown of a component — code (SVG) or the anatomy skill (principle) / exploded view (f17); an AI piece "how this works at the client's" — f04; stock — only as atmosphere |
| 5 | overlay | Stage captions, facts from product-base |
| 6 | grade, audio_mix (production sounds are valuable texture) → render → review_video | |
