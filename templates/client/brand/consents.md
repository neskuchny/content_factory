# Consents of people on camera

People may appear on camera, be inserted into AI scenes, or have their voice cloned only if there is a record here.

Status: **confirmed** (the person confirmed it themselves, in writing), **pending** (someone else said
the person agrees, or the confirmation is not written yet), **refused**, **not asked**. Only
**confirmed** allows filming; anyone who is not in this table counts as "not asked". Customers and
passers-by in the background need their own decision (blur, framing, or a sign at the shop).
