# Channel state ("where we are now")

Written by analyst at the retro from `analytics/baselines.json`. The baseline is the median of the last 20 posts
of a channel × format at the 72 h mark. What matters is the **trajectory of the baseline**, not only a post's multiplier:
a baseline that doubles in a month is a channel success, even if individual posts look "ordinary".
