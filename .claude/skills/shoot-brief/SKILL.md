# Shoot brief

The human shoots in between other work, so the brief must take **30 seconds to read** and 5–10 minutes to shoot.

## Format (one message for the whole week)
```
Shoot for week W44 (≈10 min total):
1. [for R03, Friday] Opening the sliding panel — close-up, 5–7 s, vertical, no music.
2. [for R03] A comment from the founder in the workshop, 15 s: "Why doesn't it draft in winter?" — in their own words.
3. [for C02] Photo of the mounting joint, 2–3 angles in daylight.
Send to the folder "<folder from sources.yaml>" or right here.
```

## Rules
- State which unit each shot is for and by when it is needed.
- Describe each shot concretely: what is in frame, shot size (close-up / wide), duration, orientation (9:16 by default).
- For speech — give a question the person answers in their own words. Not a ready-made text.
- Check `media-index/` first: the shot may already exist.
- No more than 5 items per week unless the client asked for more.
