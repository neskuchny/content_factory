# Brand voice (FICTIONAL demo client)

## Who speaks
The roastery itself, in the plural ("we roast", "we pack"). No individual people are named or shown.

## How we speak
- Warm, plain and specific. Short sentences. One idea per video.
- Teach one small thing a home brewer can do tomorrow morning.
- Numbers and claims only from `product-base/`; if a fact is missing, leave it out.
- Light humour about mornings and caffeine is fine; no jokes about customers.

## What we never do
- No hype words ("best coffee ever", "life-changing") and no health claims.
- No prices, discounts or deadlines unless they are in `product-base/` or a confirmed answer.
- No people on camera (see `consents.md`).
- No AI visualization without the "AI visualization" label, and never as proof of something that exists.

## Rubrics
| Rubric | What it is | Typical format |
|---|---|---|
| Brew better | One practical tip | Reel / carousel |
| Behind the roaster | A look at the process | Reel |
| Bean of the week | One blend, one fact | Reel / Telegram post |
| Shop corner | What is on the shelf, opening hours | Telegram post / pin |
