# Visual style (FICTIONAL demo client)

- Palette: espresso brown `#2B1D14`, cream `#F6EFE6`, accent copper `#C26A2E` (see `tokens.json`)
- Fonts: Fraunces (headings), Inter (body). Both cover Latin and Cyrillic.
- Logo: simple wordmark "Brewline", SVG in `brand/assets/logo.svg` (placeholder; not included in the demo)
- Subtitles: Inter Bold, cream text on a soft dark plate, accent copper for the keyword of each phrase, bottom third above the safe zone
- AI badge: "AI visualization" — permanent, legible, top-right corner, on the whole video
- Reels covers: one close-up object (beans, drum, cup), big 2–4 word title in Fraunces, lots of empty space
- Carousels: 1080×1350, cream background, one idea per slide, template in `brand/templates/carousel/`

## Prohibited
- Text and logos inside a generation (text is always added with code).
- Distorting the geometry of real products: bags, labels and equipment must look like the real ones.
- Stock images of "generic coffee" in place of the real roastery.
