# Roasting process (FICTIONAL)

Source for every line below: client interview notes, 2027-01-11 (invented for the demo).

## Steps
1. Green beans are weighed into 5 kg batches.
2. A batch is roasted in a small drum roaster on site. One roast takes about 12–15 minutes.
3. Roasted beans cool on a cooling tray for a few minutes.
4. Beans rest for 5 days before they are packed for sale.
5. Bags are filled by hand, sealed and stamped with the roast date.

## Equipment
- One 5 kg drum roaster, installed in the shop's back room.
