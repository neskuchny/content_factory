# Care tips (FICTIONAL, confirmed by the client 2027-01-11)

## Storage
- Keep beans in an airtight container, away from light and heat.
- Do not store beans in the freezer door or next to the stove.

## Freshness
- Best brewed from about 5 to 28 days after the roast date printed on the bag.
- Grind just before brewing.

## Brewing basics
- Starting ratio for filter coffee: 60 g of coffee per litre of water.
- Weigh the coffee instead of using spoons.
- Use water just off the boil (about 93 °C).
